KeralaLotto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

KeralaLotto (“the app”) displays Kerala Government Lottery results published on the official public website keralalotteries.net. It is published by Ghonestapps (developer: Geo Paulson). This policy explains what data the app handles and what its advertising partner collects.

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. It reports results of draws that have already taken place. It does not sell lottery tickets, accept money, or let you place any wager.

1. Data we collect

We collect nothing. KeralaLotto has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We operate no server of our own, so no information about you is sent to us. We cannot see which tickets you check or which results you view.

The app does contain advertising, and our advertising partner collects data of its own. That is described in section 5.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Lottery results fetched from keralalotteries.net are cached in a local database (Android Room) so results stay readable offline. Cached results are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Your language choice (Malayalam or English) and your notification preference are saved in local storage (Android DataStore).

All of this stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Ticket numbers you type or scan are used for the search you asked for and are not saved anywhere.

3. Internet access

The app connects to the internet for two purposes only: to read publicly available lottery results from keralalotteries.net, and to request advertisements (section 5). The request to keralalotteries.net is a plain read of a public page — nothing about you is included in it.

4. Camera

The camera permission is optional and is used only by the Scan feature, which reads the number printed on a lottery ticket. Recognition runs entirely on your device using Google ML Kit on-device text recognition. No photograph or camera frame is uploaded, stored, or shared — images are processed in memory and discarded. You can decline the camera permission and use the rest of the app normally by typing ticket numbers instead.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

KeralaLotto is free and supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear as a banner below the results and in the dialog shown when you leave the app.

To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level, not precise GPS location);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and interactions with the ads shown.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising terms. We receive only anonymous, aggregated performance totals — impressions, clicks and earnings. We never receive information that identifies you.

Your choices. On Android you can limit this at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely to opt out of personalised advertising. Deleting the advertising ID does not stop ads from appearing; it stops them from being personalised.

6. Notifications

The app checks once a day for newly published results and shows a local notification when it finds them. The check and the notification happen on your device; no data leaves it for this purpose. You can turn notifications off in the app’s Settings screen or in Android settings.

7. Permissions we request

  • Internet and network state — to fetch results and ads.
  • Camera (optional) — for the Scan feature described in section 4.
  • Notifications — to tell you when new results are published.

The app does not request precise location, contacts, storage, microphone, or any other sensitive permission.

8. Children

KeralaLotto is not directed at children. Its content concerns a state lottery and its target audience is users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information, and we have none to sell. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, in its capacity as the advertising provider described in section 5.
