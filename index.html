Privacy Policy — KeralaLotto

Last updated: 2026-08-26

KeralaLotto ("the app") is an offline-first Android app that displays Kerala Government Lottery results scraped from the official public website (keralalotteries.net). It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data Collection

The app does not collect any personal data, usage analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers. No account or sign-in is required.

Internet Access

The app uses internet access solely to fetch publicly available lottery results from keralalotteries.net. No user data is sent to any server.

Camera

The app requests camera permission for the optional Scan feature, which uses on-device ML Kit OCR to read lottery ticket numbers. Camera data is processed entirely on the device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Notifications

The app sends local notifications when new lottery results are published. No data leaves the device for this purpose.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Local Storage

Lottery result data is stored locally in an on-device Room database for offline access. This data is cleared automatically after 30 days. No data is backed up to the cloud.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
